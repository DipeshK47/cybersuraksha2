## Casework boundaries

All organisation names, .example domains, DEMO registry references, accounts, transactions, receipts, voices and case files are fictional. The teacher-created registry extract is not an actual regulatory lookup. No real investment product or person is evaluated. Verifying an entity/role/channel does not establish that a sender controls it or guarantee returns, safety or recovery. The classroom never installs an app, shares credentials, transfers funds or submits a complaint.

The recruitment case teaches declining stranger-directed account use and onward transfers, preserving relevant evidence and seeking trusted bank/adult support if an account was used. It does not determine liability, intent or legal guilt. The report lesson uses prompt bank/payment-provider contact and official Indian financial-cyber-fraud routes (1930 / cybercrime.gov.in), plus trusted-adult/school safeguarding help for coercion. Evidence gathering must not delay urgent help. Relevant originals remain restricted; unrelated information is redacted only in classroom copies. No PIN/OTP, harmful content or actual identity document is requested, and nothing is forwarded to a group or external recipient.

## Pattern and prevention models

The same-day, whole-rupee transfer rule flags an intermediary forwarding 90–100% of an incoming amount within the stated window. At five minutes it flags V (T02→T03, three minutes) and C (T04→T05, four minutes). An independently supplied fictional receipt explains C’s group purchase; V’s source/purpose remain unresolved. A flag is only a private context-review signal. If converted into a fraud verdict, the documented benign match would create a false accusation. Unknown truth labels do not support a meaningful precision, accuracy or false-positive-rate claim. The model validates unique transfer IDs, valid times and positive bounded integer amounts and preserves the source table.

The prevention aid has four explicit decisions: independent contact route, pause before unverified payment, prompt trusted help after loss and restricted evidence. The weak draft fails both prepared cases. The lab retains the first rules/results while the student revises and retests; editing without rerunning cannot count as a tested revision. Passing two structured cases is limited evidence. A peer/teacher separately tests whether another learner can use the guide under pressure, including a fresh ambiguous case.

The integrated case keeps separate conclusions separate: an independently confirmed ₹2,300 vendor order does not validate a ₹3,200 request to another beneficiary, authenticate a voice or remove a separately confirmed export exposure. Conflicting event content stays quarantined and unsupported assistant advice remains unsupported. Named authorised roles own containment, support, official contact and restricted evidence.

Dossier source labels link to completed Class 12 security, AI, algorithm and prevention chapters. A short reflection (up to 300 characters) and the four labels are saved locally and included in the submitted answer’s teacher-history feedback. The implementation bounds/normalises the feedback to fit the existing 2,000-character activity payload. Free text is rendered as text and is not graded by length. Earlier attempts remain visible when an unsafe decision is revised.

## Primary guidance checked 25 October 2026

- [SEBI Investor: How to Spot a Scam](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/spot-any-scam.html): guaranteed/high-return claims, pressure and independent verification. No real product recommendation is made.
- [RBI BE(A)WARE awareness booklet (2022)](https://systemhealth.rbi.org.in/cms.rbi.org.in/cms/assets/Documents/BEAWARE07032022.pdf): money-mule/account-use recruitment pattern and caution about receiving/transferring others’ funds for a commission. This educational reference is not a statement of current case-specific liability or banking procedure.
- [Navi Mumbai Police cyber-security guidance](https://navimumbaipolice.gov.in/en/citizen/cyber-security): prompt bank contact, evidence preservation, secret protection and official reporting routes.
- [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/): official Indian reporting destination for a real incident. The lesson never submits its mock material.

No reimbursement guarantee, fixed legal reporting deadline, certainty of attribution or legal conclusion is taught.
